# 投稿信（Cover Letter）模板庫

> 適用：國際期刊英文投稿信 + 台灣中文期刊投稿信

---

## 英文投稿信：標準版

```
[Date]

Dear Editor(s) of [Journal Name],

I am pleased to submit the manuscript titled "[論文完整標題]" 
for consideration for publication in [Journal Name].

[研究摘要段落 - 2-3 句]
This paper [describes/investigates/proposes/examines] [研究內容簡述]. 
Using [研究方法], we found that [主要發現一句話]. 
Our study contributes to the field by [核心貢獻].

We believe this manuscript is well-suited for [Journal Name] because 
[說明與期刊主題的契合之處].

We confirm that:
• This manuscript has not been published elsewhere.
• This manuscript is not currently under review at another journal.
• All authors have approved this submission.
• [若適用] This study received IRB approval (Protocol #: [編號]).

Thank you for your consideration. We look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,

[Corresponding Author Name]
[Title, Department, Institution]
[Email]
[Phone (optional)]
[Date]
```

## 英文投稿信：台灣研究特化版（強調台灣脈絡）

```
[Date]

Dear Dr./Prof. [Editor Name],

We are submitting our manuscript "[Title]" to [Journal Name] 
for your consideration.

[Abstract summary 2-3 sentences]

This study addresses a research gap particularly relevant to 
East Asian/Taiwanese contexts: [說明為何台灣研究值得國際關注].
While previous research has focused primarily on Western contexts, 
our findings from [台灣語料/樣本/環境] provide important cross-cultural 
insights that we believe will interest [期刊] readers.

[其餘標準聲明段落]

Sincerely,
[作者資訊]
```

## 中文投稿信（台灣中文期刊）

```
[日期]

[期刊名稱] 編輯委員會 敬啟：

本文謹以「[論文題目]」一文投稿貴刊，敬請惠予審閱。

本研究旨在[研究目的一句話]。本文以[研究方法]為主要研究途徑，
以[樣本/語料庫]為分析材料，主要發現為[主要結論一句話]。
本研究對[研究領域]的貢獻在於[核心貢獻]。

本文未曾在其他學術期刊發表，亦未同時投稿其他期刊。
全體作者均已閱覽本稿，並同意以此版本投稿。
[若適用] 本研究已通過所屬機構 IRB 倫理審查（核准編號：___）。

懇請  編輯委員會惠予考慮，謝謝。

[作者姓名] 謹上
[職稱、系所、服務機構]
[電子郵件]
[日期]
```

## 再次投稿信（Resubmission Cover Letter）

```
Dear Editor,

We are resubmitting our manuscript "[Title]" (originally submitted 
as Manuscript ID: [編號], submitted [日期]).

Based on the reviewers' comments, we have substantially revised the 
manuscript. Key revisions include:
1. [修訂重點一]
2. [修訂重點二]
3. [修訂重點三]

A detailed point-by-point response to each reviewer comment is 
attached as a separate document.

[標準聲明段落]

Thank you for the opportunity to revise and resubmit this work.

Sincerely,
[作者資訊]
```
