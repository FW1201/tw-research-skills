# 審稿回覆模板庫（Rebuttal Templates）

> 設計參考：agent-research-skills/lingzhi227（rebuttal-writing 機制）
> 本庫收錄各類型審稿意見回覆模板，分中英文版本

---

## 開場白模板庫

### 感謝類（英文）
```
1. "Thank you for your careful and constructive review of our manuscript."
2. "We are grateful for the reviewers' thorough examination and valuable suggestions."
3. "We appreciate the time and effort you have devoted to reviewing our work."
4. "The reviewers' comments have significantly helped us improve the manuscript."
5. "We thank the reviewers for their insightful observations."
```

### 感謝類（中文）
```
1. 「感謝審稿委員仔細審閱本文，並提出寶貴的修訂建議。」
2. 「審稿委員的意見對本研究的改善極具助益，謹此致謝。」
3. 「感謝委員耐心審閱，相關建議已於修訂版中逐一回應。」
```

## 類型 A：方法論質疑回應

### 樣本數不足
```
[英文]
We appreciate the reviewer's concern regarding sample size. 
We acknowledge that [X] participants may appear limited; however, 
[原因：如為罕見族群/深度質性研究/先導研究]. 

We have conducted a post-hoc power analysis which indicates 
that our sample achieves adequate power (β = [X], α = .05) 
for the key analyses (see revised manuscript, Page [X]).

We have also added explicit discussion of this limitation 
in the Limitations section (Page [X], Lines [Y-Z]).
```

### 統計方法質疑
```
[英文]
Thank you for raising this important methodological concern. 

The reviewer is correct that [問題描述]. We have addressed 
this by [修正方式：如：re-running the analysis using [新方法]].

The revised results are presented in Table [X] (Page [X]) 
and are consistent with our original findings: [結果描述].

We have updated the Method section (Page [X]) and Discussion 
(Page [X]) to reflect these changes.
```

## 類型 B：文獻補充要求

```
[英文]
Thank you for suggesting these important works. We have 
carefully read [作者, 年份] and [作者, 年份], and have 
integrated them into the revised manuscript as follows:

- [作者, 年份] is now cited in the Introduction (Page [X]) 
  where we discuss [引用脈絡].
- [作者, 年份] is incorporated into the Discussion (Page [X]) 
  to [說明整合方式].

[中文版]
感謝委員建議補充 [作者（年份）] 的相關研究。
修訂版已於第 [X] 頁補充此文獻，並說明與本研究之關聯：
[如何整合]。
```

## 類型 C：表達/格式修正

```
[英文]
We apologize for the unclear expression. We have revised 
[the sentence/paragraph/table/figure] as follows:

  Original text (Page [X], Line [Y]):
  "[原文]"

  Revised text:
  "[修改後文字]"

We believe this revision now clearly conveys [說明].

[中文版]
感謝委員指出此處表達不清楚。修訂版已修改如下：

  原文（第 [X] 頁，第 [Y] 行）：
  「[原文]」

  修訂後：
  「[修改後]」
```

## 類型 D：禮貌堅持立場

```
[英文]
We appreciate the reviewer's thoughtful perspective on [主題].

We have carefully considered this suggestion and, after 
deliberation, have decided to maintain our original approach 
for the following reasons:

1. [理由一：理論依據]
2. [理由二：方法一致性]
3. [理由三：前人文獻支持]

We have, however, added a more explicit acknowledgment of 
this alternative view in the Discussion section (Page [X], 
Lines [Y-Z]) to ensure readers are aware of this perspective.

We hope the reviewer finds this explanation satisfactory.

[中文版]
感謝委員提出此一觀點。本研究考量後，仍維持原有設計，
原因如下：
1. [理由]
2. [理由]

但已於第 [X] 頁討論此替代觀點，以供讀者參考。
```

## 拒絕並感謝（最終拒稿後備用）

```
如期刊最終做出拒稿決定，可準備感謝信：

Dear Dr./Prof. [Editor Name],

Thank you for the constructive review process for our manuscript 
"[Title]." While we are disappointed by the decision, we 
genuinely appreciate the reviewers' detailed comments, which 
have helped us significantly improve the work.

We plan to revise the manuscript based on the reviewers' 
suggestions and submit to another venue.

Thank you again for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
[作者]
```
